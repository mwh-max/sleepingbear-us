Let's Work Together

I work with nonprofits, public health organizations, libraries, and civic institutions that have a problem a well-built, lightweight tool could address. If that sounds like your organization, I'd like to hear about it.

I'm not an agency. I'm one person. That means direct communication, a clear scope, and a finished product — with a full account of what I built and why.

How it works

  1. Short conversation about the problem
  2. A clearly scoped proposal — no retainers, no feature creep
  3. A finished tool, with notes on every decision made along the way

Current focus areas

Public Health Access · Food Insecurity · Civic Data · Field Safety · Plain-Language Guides
